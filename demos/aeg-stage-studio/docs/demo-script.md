# Stage Studio · demo script (about 3 minutes)

Follows deck slide 6 (“Creative workflow, step by step”) and lands on slide 5’s value to prove. Before you start:
open http://localhost:5173, click **Reset the demo** (bottom of the projects panel), and set the window to 1440 px wide
or more. Run it once on the presenting laptop: the previews are rendered live and use its graphics card.

| Time | Step (slide 6) | Do | Say |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Frame it | Open **Demo** in the header for a second. | “A working name for an AEG workspace on Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform. Nothing here calls a model yet; the previews are illustrative.” |
| 0:15 | **1 · Brief** | **New project**. Answer with the suggestion chips. | “The artist, or their team, describes the show. Gemini turns mood boards, the set list and the venue specs into a brief: watch it fill in.” |
| 0:50 | | **Start project**. Open the agent’s reasoning in the chat. | “Gemini drafts the prompts. Here is exactly what it asked Nano Banana Pro for.” |
| 1:05 | **2 · Stills** | Switch to **[Artist A] · Coachella 2027**. Set *Time of day* to Night. On B1, open the “1 to look at” badge, then **Annotate**: click the top of the monolith, type “sharper top edge”, add “warmer overall”, **Apply 2 changes**. **Compare with v1**, then **Select** B1. | “Several concepts per round, seen at the time of day and crowd the artist will actually play to, like the Coachella prototype with DeepMind. The agent checks each against the venue and the brief; you mark what should change right on the image, and every version is kept.” |
| 1:35 | **3 · Motion** | **Put into motion**, pick song 14, play. Type “slow the split” in the chat. | “Veo 3.1 for motion: lighting, LED content and the camera move per song. Edits are in plain words, with Gemini Omni Flash.” |
| 2:05 | **4 · Review** | **Send to artist**. Turn on **Preview as artist**, click the image to pin a comment, then **Approve** and give 4 stars. | “The artist reviews stills and motion together and signs off. Iterate until they do.” |
| 2:35 | **Handoff** | Land on Handoff. Point at *Value to prove*, then *Rights and provenance*. | “The three measures we’d prove in the residency: concept rounds, time to an approved design, artist feedback. Artist owns the outputs; SynthID on everything; legal confirms C2PA and indemnity scope.” |
| 2:55 | Close | Jump to **[Artist B] · The O2** in the projects panel. | “And a service AEG Presents can run for many artists at once: festivals, arenas, tours.” |

If something stalls: every step can be opened directly from the progress rail on the right, and the three sample
projects are already at Review ([Artist B]) and Handoff ([Artist C]).
