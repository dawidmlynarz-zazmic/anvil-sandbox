# Stage Studio · demo script (about 3 minutes)

Follows deck slide 6 (“Creative workflow, step by step”) and lands on slide 5’s value to prove. The show is designed
the way an artist thinks about it: one key moment (a song) at a time, on one shared set. Before you start: open
http://localhost:5173 and click **Reset the demo** (bottom of the projects panel). Set the window to 1440 px wide or
more, and run it once on the presenting laptop: the previews are rendered live and use its graphics card.

| Time | Step (slide 6) | Do | Say |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Frame it | The app opens on **All projects**. Open **Demo** in the header for a second. | “A working name for an AEG workspace on Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform. Nothing here calls a model yet; the previews are illustrative, and the artists are fictional. Every show AEG is designing sits here.” |
| 0:15 | **1 · Brief** | Open **Juniper Cole · Stagecoach 2027**. In the chat, answer the three opening questions: **Mane Stage**, **Sunset into dark**, the first look, then **Submit**; then **Nothing beyond the venue rules**. Point at the venue facts and the daylight timeline. | “Three quick choices set up most of the brief. The venues are AEG’s own, with what AEG publishes about them; the specs are samples until the venue library is connected. The slot sets the light: this set starts at golden hour and ends in the dark.” |
| 0:45 | | **Build the show**. Open the agent’s reasoning in the chat. | “The agent drafts the key moments from the setlist and starts on the first one. Here is exactly what it asked Nano Banana Pro for.” |
| 1:00 | **2 · Explore a moment** | Song 3 gets four directions. Open #2 (the monolith), **More like #2**, pin two you like, tick three and **Compare**. On one, open its “to look at” badge, then type “narrower, warmer” in the chat. | “Many images, not four: batches of new directions, more like this, other angles, other light. Every change is a new image with its lineage, so nothing is lost and anything can be branched.” |
| 1:35 | **3 · Look and set** | **Choose #… as the look**. Its clip starts rendering; play it. **Lock the set from #…**. Go to **Next: song 7** and **Explore four looks**. | “One set for the whole show, a look per moment. Locking the set keeps the structure for every song; from now on only the light, colour and haze change. Each look gets its eight-second Veo clip, in the light that song plays in.” |
| 1:55 | **Asset kit** | Back on song 3, open **Assets** and **Build the asset kit**. Open one asset’s **Provenance**. | “From one look, everything production and content teams build from: every camera position, every light state, other camera moves. Each asset says which model made it, from what, and what protects it.” |
| 2:05 | **4 · Review** | Open **The Velvet Static · The O2** from the sidebar and click **Preview the show**: the four moments play in order. Go to Review: the artist replied. Go through the tabs per moment, then **Request changes**. On the show board, songs 2 and 6 show new images with the comments applied; choose new looks and **Send the new version**. | “The artist comments per moment, on the look or the clip. Their notes go straight into new images for those moments. One approval covers the whole show.” |
| 2:35 | **Handoff** | Open **Ren Halloway · Crypto.com Arena** (approved). Point at the set, the moments grid, the cue sheet, then *Value to prove*. | “Production gets the set, every moment’s look and clip, the cue sheet and the decision log. The three measures we’d prove: reviews with the artist, days to an approved show, artist feedback.” |
| 2:55 | Close | Back to **All projects**. | “A service AEG Presents can run for many artists at once: festivals, arenas, residencies.” |

If something stalls: every step opens from the progress rail on the right (each key moment is listed under Show), and
the sample projects are further along: Marisol Vey exploring song 3, The Velvet Static at Review with the artist’s
comments, Ren Halloway at Handoff.
