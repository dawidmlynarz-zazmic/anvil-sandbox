# Stage Studio · demo script (about 3 minutes)

Follows deck slide 6 (“Creative workflow, step by step”) and lands on the handoff. The show is designed
the way an artist thinks about it: one key moment (a song) at a time. Before you start: open
http://localhost:5173 and click **Reset the demo** (bottom of the projects panel). Set the window to 1440 px wide or
more, and run it once on the presenting laptop: the previews are rendered live and use its graphics card.

| Time | Step (slide 6) | Do | Say |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Frame it | The app opens on **All projects**. Open **Demo** in the header for a second. | “A working name for an AEG workspace on Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform. Nothing here calls a model yet; the previews are illustrative, and the artists are fictional. Every show AEG is designing sits here.” |
| 0:15 | **1 · Brief** | Open **Juniper Cole · Stagecoach 2027**. In the chat, answer the three opening questions: **Mane Stage**, **Sunset into dark**, the first look, then **Submit**; then **Nothing beyond the venue rules**. Point at the venue facts and the daylight timeline. | “Three quick choices set up most of the brief. The venues are AEG’s own, with what AEG publishes about them; the specs are samples until the venue library is connected. The slot sets the light: this set starts at golden hour and ends in the dark.” |
| 0:45 | | **Generate show** (top right): the show board opens with the drafted songs. Open Gemini’s reasoning in the chat. | “Gemini drafts the key moments from the setlist and starts on the first one. Here is exactly what it asked Nano Banana Pro for.” |
| 1:00 | **2 · Explore a moment** | Open song 1: it gets four directions. Open #2 (the monolith), then **More like #2** from the Explore menu. Open #2’s issues badge: the wind and sightline checks, with numbers. Type “narrower” in the chat: the new image passes both. | “Many images, not four: batches of new directions, more like this, other angles, other light. Every image is checked against the venue: here the monolith would need a wind plan at Coachella’s evening gusts, and ‘narrower’ fixes it. Every change is a new image with its lineage.” |
| 1:35 | **3 · Motion** | **Generate motion** on the open image: its clip leads the page; play it. Go to song 2 with the arrow at the top, type a description in its prompt (e.g. “warmer, more haze”) and send it. | “A look per moment, each checked against the venue. Each look gets its eight-second Veo clip, in the light that song plays in.” |
| 2:05 | **4 · Handoff** | Open **The Velvet Static · The O2** from the sidebar: every song is in motion. Click **Preview**: the four moments play in order. Then **Generate handoff**. | “When every song is in motion, the show goes straight to production. One click puts the package together for each team.” |
| 2:35 | | Click through **Staging and rigging**, **Lighting** and **Camera**, then **Edit show**: change a song’s look and **Generate handoff** again (v2). Open **Rights** for the credits. | “Each trade gets its own view: the stage builder each look’s footprint and venue checks, the lighting designer each moment’s light, camera its moves. Changes later on make a new version of the handoff, not a new project. The credits say who decided what: the team chose every look, Gemini explored.” |
| 2:55 | Close | Back to **All projects**. | “A service AEG Presents can run for many artists at once: festivals, arenas, residencies.” |

Throughout, the badge in the header shows where the project stands (In progress or Handed off), and the show board
keeps the running numbers in one line. If something stalls: every moment opens from the show board, and the sample
projects are further along: Marisol Vey exploring song 1, The Velvet Static with every song in motion, Ren Halloway
handed off.
