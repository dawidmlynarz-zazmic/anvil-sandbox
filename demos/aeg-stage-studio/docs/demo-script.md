# Stage Studio · demo script (about 3 minutes)

Follows deck slide 6 (“Creative workflow, step by step”) and lands on slide 5’s value to prove. The show is designed
the way an artist thinks about it: one key moment (a song) at a time. Before you start: open
http://localhost:5173 and click **Reset the demo** (bottom of the projects panel). Set the window to 1440 px wide or
more, and run it once on the presenting laptop: the previews are rendered live and use its graphics card.

| Time | Step (slide 6) | Do | Say |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Frame it | The app opens on **All projects**. Open **Demo** in the header for a second. | “A working name for an AEG workspace on Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform. Nothing here calls a model yet; the previews are illustrative, and the artists are fictional. Every show AEG is designing sits here.” |
| 0:15 | **1 · Brief** | Open **Juniper Cole · Stagecoach 2027**. In the chat, answer the three opening questions: **Mane Stage**, **Sunset into dark**, the first look, then **Submit**; then **Nothing beyond the venue rules**. Point at the venue facts and the daylight timeline. | “Three quick choices set up most of the brief. The venues are AEG’s own, with what AEG publishes about them; the specs are samples until the venue library is connected. The slot sets the light: this set starts at golden hour and ends in the dark.” |
| 0:45 | | **Generate show**. Open the agent’s reasoning in the chat. | “The agent drafts the key moments from the setlist and starts on the first one. Here is exactly what it asked Nano Banana Pro for.” |
| 1:00 | **2 · Explore a moment** | Song 1 gets four directions. Open #2 (the monolith), then **More like #2** from the Explore menu. Open #2’s issues badge: the wind and sightline checks, with numbers. Type “narrower” in the chat: the new image passes both. | “Many images, not four: batches of new directions, more like this, other angles, other light. Every image is checked against the venue: here the monolith would need a wind plan at Coachella’s evening gusts, and ‘narrower’ fixes it. Every change is a new image with its lineage.” |
| 1:35 | **3 · Motion** | **Put #… into motion**: its clip leads the page; play it. Go to **Next: song 2**, type a description in its prompt (e.g. “warmer, more haze”) and send it. | “A look per moment, each checked against the venue. Each look gets its eight-second Veo clip, in the light that song plays in.” |
| 1:55 | **Asset kit** | Back on song 1, open **Asset kit** and **Build the asset kit**. Open one asset’s **Provenance**. | “From one look, everything production and content teams build from: every camera position, every light state, other camera moves. Each asset says which model made it, from what, and what protects it.” |
| 2:05 | **4 · Review** | Open **The Velvet Static · The O2** from the sidebar and click **Preview the show**: the four moments play in order. Go to Review: the artist replied. Go through the tabs per moment, then **Request changes**. On the show board, the songs with comments show new images with the comments applied; choose new looks and **Send for review** again. | “The artist comments per moment, on the look or the clip. Their notes go straight into new images for those moments. One approval covers the whole show.” |
| 2:35 | **Handoff** | Open **Ren Halloway · Crypto.com Arena** (approved). Point at *Value to prove* and *Credits*, then click through **Staging and rigging**, **Lighting** and **Camera**. | “Each trade gets its own view: the stage builder each look’s footprint and venue checks, the lighting designer each moment’s light states, camera its angles. The credits say who decided what: the team chose every look, the artist approved, the agent explored. The three measures we’d prove: reviews, days to an approved show, artist feedback.” |
| 2:55 | Close | Back to **All projects**. | “A service AEG Presents can run for many artists at once: festivals, arenas, residencies.” |

Throughout, the steps in the header show where the project stands, and the show board keeps the running numbers in
one line. If something stalls: every step opens from the header, every moment from the show board, and
the sample projects are further along: Marisol Vey exploring song 1, The Velvet Static at Review with the artist’s
comments, Ren Halloway at Handoff.
