# Great Wolf Lodge Colorado Springs: what the lodge offers

Read from greatwolf.com/colorado-springs on 2026-11-10, for the Trip Builder. Prices change often; anything shown
in the demo as a listed price was seen on that date. Flags: **C** confirmed on the lodge's own pages, **G** general GWL
page only, **N** not found. Not found means the demo shows `[price]` or "To confirm with the lodge".

## Included with a suite (C)
- Water park passes for every registered guest, from opening on arrival day until closing on departure day.
- Check-in 4pm, check-out 11am. Late check-out to 2pm is a paid add-on (price not listed).
- Free daily kids' activities: Epic Story Time, Yoga Tails, character meet and greets, arts and crafts, and a nightly
  family dance party. Life jackets and towels; wolf ears for guests 12 and under.
- Parking is extra: $20 a night plus tax.

## Passes (C, "packages vary by lodge"; no prices listed)
- **Paw Pass**: MagiQuest, a keepsake, treats and Northern Lights Arcade games.
- **Wolf Pass**: top attractions and treats, all ages. MagiQuest and Howlers Peak Ropes Course pages list it.
- **Pup Pass**: Build-A-Bear, treats, an arcade card and a souvenir, for little ones.
- Also listed: Adventure Pass, Ultimate Food & Fun Package (from $299), Splashin' Fun, cabanas.
- Ultimate Paw Pass: N. Which pass covers Ten Paw Alley: unconfirmed.

## Attractions à la carte (C)
- MagiQuest: game $14.99, wand $19.99 to $29.99, game and wand $34.99.
- Howlers Peak Ropes Course: $14.99 a climb, or $44.99 for a family of up to 6. 48in climbs alone; 42 to 48in with an
  adult.
- Also paid: Ten Paw Alley, Rustic Ridge Climbing Wall, Oliver's Mining Co., Northern Lights Arcade, Howl in One Mini
  Golf, Build-A-Bear Workshop.

## Dining (C)
- Venues: Loose Moose Family Kitchen (breakfast, dinner), Barnwood, Hungry As A Wolf (pizza, pasta), Buckets Burgers &
  Bites (water park), The Watering Hole, The Outpost, Dunkin', Wood's End Creamery, Freshwoods Market.
- Plans: Platinum Dining Credit ($150 to spend, from $120), Gold Dining Credit ($100 to spend, from $85), Wolf Wakeup
  Breakfast (all you can eat, per person per day, price not listed).
- Which venues take the dining credits and where the breakfast is served: not stated (N).
- Not at this lodge: Lodge Wood Fired Grill, Buckets Warehouse Bar & Grille, Ben & Jerry's.

## Birthdays (C, booked with a stay; no prices listed)
- **Birthday Bash**: pizza, cake, balloons and an arcade card.
- **Birthday Fun**: cake, balloons and a door decoration.
- **Birthday Basics**: an arcade card and a special balloon.
- Group parties: 10 guests minimum, 1 adult per 4 children.

## Water park (C)
Howlin' Tornado, Wolf Tail, Alberta Falls, Mountain Edge Raceway, Fort Mackenzie with Totem Towers, Whooping Hollow,
Big Foot Pass, Slap Tail Pond, Chinook Cove, Cub Paw Pool (toddlers). Kept at 84°F.

## Near the lodge (third-party, from the lodge's Things To Do page)
Garden of the Gods, Cheyenne Mountain Zoo, Pikes Peak and Barr Trail, Manitou Springs, Colorado Wolf and Wildlife
Center, U.S. Olympic & Paralympic Training Center, U.S. Air Force Academy, Old Colorado City, Glen Eyrie Castle,
Royal Gorge Bridge & Park.

## Where the demo differs from the site, on purpose
- **Rates:** the Standard and Flexible rates, and member versus non-member prices, come from the Figma and the build
  script. The lodge's suites page names no rate types; it sells a Flex Trip add-on (from $34.99) and an Early Saver
  deposit. Worth checking with the client before the demo.
- **Suites:** the lodge lists 12 suites; the demo shows three of them.
